This example draws horizontal and vertical rulers, in centimetres with
millimetre ticks and in inches with ticks every 1/16 inch.  Hold a
physical ruler against them to check that a viewer's "actual size"
zoom shows the page at its true physical size, and that it still does
after the window moves to a monitor with a different resolution.  The
hairline ticks also show up resampling artifacts: at a settled zoom,
every tick should be visible and evenly drawn.
