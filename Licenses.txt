font/cmap/predefined/*.gz
-------------------------

    gzip-compressed.  Each file carries Adobe's copyright and licence text in
    its own header and records its own version in a %%Version comment.
Upstream:    https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/cmap-resources , tag 20230118
    (commit accdf06a1aee73ce53be2e0c1ff14fb2a2b96cdd).  The files are
    unmodified apart from the compression, and LICENSE.md is an unmodified
    copy.
Copyright:   Copyright 1990-2019 Adobe.  All rights reserved.  (19 of the
    files give the years as 1990-2022.)
Licence:     BSD 3-Clause License.
Full text:   font/cmap/predefined/LICENSE.md

Copyright:   Copyright 2009, 2011, 2012 and 2025 The Go Authors.  All rights
    reserved (the year of each file is given in its header).
Licence:     BSD 3-Clause License.
Full text:   internal/filter/dct/jpeg/Licenses.txt, and in the header of each
    file.
